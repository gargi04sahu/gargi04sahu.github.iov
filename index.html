About Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting systems, networks, applications, and sensitive information from unauthorized access and cyber threats.

Common Cyber Threats

Phishing

Fake emails or websites designed to steal credentials and sensitive information.

Malicious Links

Links that redirect users to malicious websites or attempt to exploit vulnerabilities.

QR Code Scams

Malicious QR codes can redirect users to phishing pages or fraudulent websites.

Social Engineering

Attackers manipulate users into revealing confidential information or performing unsafe actions.

Stay Protected

  • Verify URLs before opening them.
  • Never share passwords or OTPs.
  • Use multi-factor authentication.
  • Keep your software updated.
  • Be careful when scanning unknown QR codes.
